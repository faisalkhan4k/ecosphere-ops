MUNICIPAL WATER CONSERVATION & EMERGENCY PROTOCOL 2026

SECTION 1: OVERVIEW
This document outlines standard operating procedures for the EcoSphere smart grid infrastructure. All automated systems and engineering agents must adhere to these guidelines.

SECTION 2: SECTOR 4 INFRASTRUCTURE (WATER SUPPLY)
Sector_4 handles the residential water distribution network for the northern quadrant.
Main isolation valves for Sector_4:
- Primary Flow Valve: VAL-SEC4-PRIME
- Auxiliary Bypass Valve: VAL-SEC4-BYPASS

SECTION 3: ANOMALY HANDLING AND EMERGENCY RESPONSE
In the event of a catastrophic drop in pressure (defined as pressure falling below 1.5 bar) combined with an abnormal spike in flow rate within Sector_4, the system is experiencing a critical structural pipe fracture.

REQUIRED ACTION PROTOCOL FOR AGENTS:
1. Issue an immediate digital isolation command to close the primary valve: VAL-SEC4-PRIME. This stops widespread flooding.
2. Open the auxiliary bypass valve within 45 seconds: VAL-SEC4-BYPASS. This reroutes emergency water supplies to the local medical clinic nearby.
3. Broadcast a repair dispatch notification containing the system code: SYS_CMD_EMERGENCY_REPAIR_S4.

SECTION 4: CONTACTS & COMPLIANCE
All overrides must be logged to the central database for municipal auditing. Environmental protection compliance code: ENV-2026-N4.
